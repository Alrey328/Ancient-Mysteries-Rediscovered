Privacy & membership

AMR Membership Privacy Notice

Version 2026-11-05-v1 · prepared for preview review

AMR membership is free and optional. You can watch every Cinema film without joining.

Information AMR stores

  • Your Google account subject identifier in keyed, pseudonymous form.
  • Your verified email address, display name, and optional Google profile image.
  • Secure session records, member likes, comments, reports, and notification choices.
  • Delivery status for emails that you explicitly requested.

AMR does not store your Google password, Google access token, or Google refresh token.

How information is used

Member data is used only to maintain your account, authorize likes and comments, moderate community content, honor notification choices, prevent abuse, and send opted-in release emails.

Service providers

Google supplies sign-in identity assertions. Cloudflare runs the AMR API, abuse protection, and D1 database. Resend delivers opted-in email and retains delivery records according to its service policy. Existing earlier discussions remain hosted separately by GitHub and Giscus.

Notification consent

Every email category is unchecked by default. You can change individual categories in My AMR or use the unsubscribe control in any email. Joining never subscribes you automatically, and AMR does not send earlier releases as a backlog.

Retention and deletion

Active account information is retained while membership remains open. Rate-limit buckets expire automatically. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, sessions, member likes, comments, reports, preferences, and queued delivery records from AMR. Anonymous qualified-view totals and pre-membership anonymous engagement cannot reliably be connected to your member identity and remain aggregated.

Your controls

My AMR lets you edit your display name, manage notification choices, sign out, and delete your account. Account deletion requires entry of the signed-in email address.

Community safety

AMR applies rate limits, automated bot checks, reporting, and moderator review. See the community guidelines.
