AMR community

Explore boldly. Discuss respectfully.

AMR welcomes curiosity, disagreement, source criticism, and imaginative interpretation. It does not welcome attacks on people.

Keep discussion constructive

  • Address ideas and evidence, not another member's identity or character.
  • Do not post harassment, threats, hate speech, sexually explicit material, or private information.
  • Do not impersonate others or repeatedly post promotional links.
  • Label speculation clearly and avoid presenting fabricated evidence as verified history.
  • Keep comments relevant to the film or investigation.

Moderation

Members can report comments. AMR moderators may hide content that violates these guidelines, preserve an audit record, and restrict abusive activity. Members can always delete their own comments.

Earlier GitHub discussions

Existing Giscus and GitHub Discussions content remains under GitHub's policies and moderation controls. It is not copied into the AMR member database.
