How to read the map

Real places. Ancient claims. Evidence kept separate.

Each qualified location connects an ancient textual tradition with its best-supported modern identification, documentary photography, archaeology, sources, and an explicit record of what the evidence does—and does not—establish.

Modern orientation: the eastern Mediterranean, including Israel, Jordan and Egypt. Ancient orientation: period-specific geography such as Canaan and Philistia will be layered into the evidence graph as the map expands.
