002

Rabbah of the Ammonites · Modern Amman, Jordan

The city where the text places Og's bedstead

Deuteronomy 3:11 places an iron bedstead associated with Og, king of Bashan, at Rabbah of the Ammonites. Ancient Rabbath-Ammon is associated with modern Amman; the Citadel preserves archaeological remains spanning the Bronze and Iron Ages and later periods.

Ancient textual claim

Deuteronomy 3:11 describes the object as nine cubits long and four cubits wide and says it was at Rabbah of the Ammonites.

Real location

Rabbath-Ammon is ancient Amman. The Amman Citadel is an archaeological anchor for the city's long occupation history.

Reconstruction

Using an 18-inch cubit gives about 13.5 × 6 feet (4.1 × 1.8 m). Our bed visualization is an artist's reconstruction; the original object has not been identified.

Evidence rule The text's dimensions are evidence for what the text claims—not archaeological proof that Og's bedstead has been found.
